# Five-hour SIMULATED scheduling experiment

> **Simulated data.** The workload, the drift and every outcome in this report are generated by documented models. Nothing is a quantum-device measurement, nothing replays the recorded dataset as new evidence, and no hardware or scientific-improvement claim is made.

## Setup

- Workload fingerprint `3ec7ed848e6bde6191e8618ed033717c78c03398a2f925fbe394836f1722d328`; 8256 requests (16512 shots) over 18000 s of simulated device time.
- Device (ASSUMED, not measured): 1.0 s per slot, 18000 slots = 5.00 device-hours; capacity 0.50 requests/s (a pair needs two slots); mean offered load 0.90; overloaded blocks [2, 5, 6, 10, 13].
- Registry: 15 eligible pairs of 91 (content sha256 `9204db00e4829446...`).
- Policies: arrival-order, consecutive-pair, drift-aware, drift-aware-age. The default policy is `consecutive-pair`.
- Drift: simulated, rate factors [1.0, 10.0, 100.0, 1000.0] x 5 drift realisations. Factor 1 is the fitted-scale slow drift; larger factors are synthetic stress scenarios.
- Outcomes are drawn at the actual execution times, `Binomial(repetitions, p_k(t_exec))`, with common random numbers across policies.

Error definitions: `E_AB` = |p_A(t_A) - p_B(t_B) - (p_A(t_ready) - p_B(t_ready))| (drift-induced, continuous); `E_sync` uses the comparison's own earlier execution time as reference; `E_outcome` uses the generated binomial outcomes and includes shot noise. Reliable = `E_AB <=` level (0.05 is the project's pre-registered level; 0.01 and 0.02 are labelled sensitivity levels).

## Limitations

- SIMULATED workload and SIMULATED drift: no value here is a quantum-device measurement and no recorded observation is replayed.
- The device service time per slot is an assumed parameter, not measured hardware capacity; one slot is one logical job whatever its repetition count.
- The drift fluctuation spectrum is assumed (circuit response constants are the project's fitted ones). Drift rate factors above 1 are labelled stress scenarios.
- The automated registry decisions were generated using held-out recorded observations; this experiment cannot validate them.
- The recorded-data drift-priority persistence validation was inconclusive. Priorities here are learned from a simulated pre-horizon campaign and are not validated against real drift.
- Mean errors over completed comparisons depend on WHICH requests a policy completed; use the paired differences (requests completed under both policies).
- One workload realisation and a small number of drift realisations; intervals reflect time blocks within a run, not workload-to-workload variation.
