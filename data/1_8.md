# Scheduling quantum comparisons under device drift

## Papers 

1. Ravi et al. Navigating the Dynamic Noise Landscape of Variational Quantum Algorithms with QISMET. ASPLOS 2023.

Build a scheduler for groups of quantum experiments that must be compared, such as the two measurements used to estimate a gradient. 
The scheduler will run related samples close together and on suitable device locations so that changing device noise causes less bias. 
It will also track the uncertainty of the final comparison. 
Implement the scheduler, test it with recorded or controlled drift, and measure whether it completes more reliable comparisons within the same device time.

Download the dataset from https://zenodo.org/records/4033077 and open ramsey/experiment/results/data/dataset.txt. 
It contains fourteen Ramsey circuit sequences, their measurement timestamps and their binary outcomes. The circuits differ in the waiting time between two pulses. Using the timestamps, divide the measurements into the same clock-time windows for every circuit, such as consecutive ten-minute intervals. 
Within each window, calculate the fraction of outcomes equal to 1 and an uncertainty interval that accounts for the number of measurements. 
Then compare how these fractions change between windows. 
Two circuits can have different average outcomes yet experience the same drift. 
For example, a change from 0.4 to 0.5 in one circuit and from 0.6 to 0.7 in another represents the same increase of 0.1.Check whether differences between these changes exceed what measurement uncertainty can explain. 
Identify groups for which a shared correction is supported, and test those groups on later observations excluded from fitting. 
Build a queue simulator to compare your proposed scheduler with arrival-order scheduling and running related experiments consecutively. 
Give each method the same measurement budget and measure comparison error, uncertainty estimates, waiting time and completed comparisons.
